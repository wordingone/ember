- at least 3,000,000,000 total unique stored neural parameters;
- clean-genesis architecture, data, tokenizer, update, and checkpoint lineage;
- sufficient training demonstrated by heldout capability, not a smoke run;
- native text, image, audio, reasoning, and structured tool use in one decoder;
- exact total, trainable, active, and trained-capacity accounting;
- checkpoint-bound reasoning and modality evidence;
- no borrowed learned or evaluative signal; and

The same floor, without regression or capability deferral, binds 7B, 15B, and
>27B rungs.
