# Lab domain

Lab owns cross-domain experiments, integration, and operator-visible experimental composition. It
does not replace the authority of Model, Data, Training, Evaluation, Runtime, Infrastructure, or
Governance.

The target `ember.lab` interface is declared. Lab may integrate all seven other domains through
their declared boundaries. An integrated experiment remains distinct from a reviewed evaluation,
an admitted model, or a governance verdict.

Smallest safe path: use the CPU-only receipt checker in the
[verification guide](../../guides/VERIFY.md). Operator procedures are indexed under
[`docs/operator/`](../../operator/); follow current resource authority before any service or GPU
launch.
