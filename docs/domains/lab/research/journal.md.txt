# Ember #1945 research journal and experiment log

A live record of what the #1945 throughput-and-qualification campaign is trying, what each attempt
showed, and what it did not close. Its purpose is to make the next decision better than the last
one. Read it before proposing any arm, and write to it every time an experiment is ruled.

- **Campaign ledger** (the full history to 2026-09-27, by gate, with repeat detection):
  [campaign.html](campaign.html), source `campaign.md`.
- **Experiment records** (every loop hypothesis, frozen criterion and verdict): [hypotheses.html](hypotheses.html).
- The log below is appended automatically when a loop hypothesis is ruled or updated. Hand entries
  record decisions, corrections and anything a loop stage does not capture.

## How to use this before deciding

1. Search the log and the ledger for the proposition you are about to test, **in its own words**.
   An empty search is not clearance.
2. Check the **Standing lessons**. Each one names a mistake this campaign made more than once.
3. State which gate the experiment moves, its ceiling, and its frozen refutation criterion. Then run it.
4. After the ruling, check that the entry landed here. Add what the result does **not** close.

## Standing lessons

1. **An instrument is proven before its verdict is trusted.** From attempt 8 to 2026-09-27, every
   missing-image and wrong-image MMMU control ran an evaluator that ignored the image condition. The
   conclusion "the image rarely moves MMMU" was never measured. A control run must record the
   condition it actually executed.
2. **A claim is made by the acceptance consumer**, never by the statistic the arm was built to move.
   Several clearances of 80,000 were read from a row timer that covered only part of each update.
3. **Fixing one gate has repeatedly broken another.** Attempts 7 to 9 met A and B but failed D.
   The v3 mixture that brought D closest (attempts 12 to 14) lost A and B. Judge every attempt on all
   six gates.
4. **Isolated probes overpredict governed gains** (by 5× or more on this campaign). Size a treatment
   with a matched captured pair, null pair first.
5. **No attempt is dispatched while known to fail a gate.** Attempts 11 to 14 were, four times, on
   an unchanged arm.
