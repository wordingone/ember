Ember

Ember is a from-scratch language model trained on one RTX 4090. Everything it knows was learned on that single card, under a fixed accounting contract: every trained token is receipted, every checkpoint is reproducible, and no result counts unless an artifact a third party can open says it happened.

The constraint is the experiment. One consumer card is a hard budget, so every question becomes a measurement question -- where the time actually goes, and what would have to change for it to go somewhere else.
