## Shared contract applied independently to every domain

Every A-K domain retains all of these requirements independently:

- L3 forbids external-model generation, filtering, ranking, scoring, and
  selection.
- L4 requires exact source identity, canonical URL, pinned revision or commit,
  exact bytes and hashes, license verdict, acquisition timestamp, transform
  lineage, manifest path, and producer receipt custody.
- L5 admits only free/public sources without agent-created accounts. Account-
  or consent-gated sources remain `PIN-PENDING` with the exact blocker named.
  Public-domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, ODC-By, Apache, MIT, and BSD sources may
  be admitted after verification. Terms must be verified at fetch time and the
  license verdict recorded; NC, ND, and custom-research terms remain
  `LICENSE-GATE`, and every unknown or unresolved source is excluded.
- Exact-hash and local MinHash-class near-deduplication must run within and
  across domains.
- Pinned-evaluation decontamination must include n-gram checks and bind exact
  test hashes before any train-split admission. #440 remains the separate open
  owner of its unresolved v2.1 predicate; no A-K domain may borrow completion
  from it.
- Each domain must retain its required independent textbook, paper, reference,
  applied, or domain-specific registers.
- Receipts, not assertions, authorize admission. The canonical board's data
  conditions must consume the exact acquisition receipts. Missing or mutated
  bytes, hashes, licenses, producer receipts, validation results, or foreign
  receipt families fail closed.

The shared schema is safe only as a per-domain acquisition ledger. No row may
inherit another domain's source bytes, receipt, license verdict, integrity
check, diversity register, or closure state.
