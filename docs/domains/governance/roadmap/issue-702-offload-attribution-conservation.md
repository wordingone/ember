# Issue #702 terminal conservation ruling

Status: `SUPERSEDED_NOT_PLANNED` for the historical 2.2B offload-attribution
vehicle. The surviving current-scale optimizer and memory-traffic obligations
are conserved by canonical issue #707 and the EMBER-02 contract.

Source master: `4d814bbec3116ea0eb9d16298a7747c043828727`.

## Historical scope and evidence boundary

Issue #702 specified a step-time and PCIe attribution experiment on the former
2.2B rung-2 CPU-offload path. Its frozen amendments required production-path
fwd/loss/bwd and optimizer subphase timing, exact transfer bytes, pageable and
pinned calibration, counterbalanced profiled/unprofiled blocks, wall closure,
resource and interval-tenancy evidence, and an executable five-verdict grammar.
Later amendments added a matched cure A/B and a major-reduction bar.

The public record contains useful historical evidence, but not the issue's
terminal result. Two early live receipts executed a substituted 434M subject
and were explicitly demoted. Attempt 3 did bind the historical 2.2B realized
parameter count and its raw arrays independently reproduce combined
`inner_optimizer_step` dominance: mean ratio `0.9170657781`, bootstrap CI95
`[0.9164403372, 0.9177304423]`, about `55.5957514` seconds per profiled step.
That arithmetic is retained as historical measurement only.

The same independent audit established why the result is not contract-grade
branch selection. The combined span aggregates Muon/NS5, other Muon work,
AdamW work, and file-backed traffic; it cannot evaluate the frozen NS5-specific
Branch A predicate. The receipt also lacks executing source hashes and the
current interval-tenancy proof. No matched cure A/B established the frozen
major-reduction bar: at least 10x reduction of the targeted component, at least
2x end-to-end tokens/second at lower confidence bound, matched loss,
update-survival, and VRAM compliance.

No current optimizer verdict, cure result, speedup, GPU result, training result,
checkpoint result, capability result, or completed 2.2B experiment is claimed
by this ruling.

## Why the named vehicle is obsolete

The experiment's named subject is the historical 2.2B/cbase rung. Current
`GOAL.md` and `docs/domains/governance/roadmap/milestones/EMBER-02.md` require the first admissible
Ember research network to contain at least 3,000,000,000 unique parameters and
make smaller artifacts read-only history. The old offload consumer, checkpoint,
optimizer split, schedule, byte ledger, and 79--81 second price therefore cannot
authorize a present experiment or be silently relabeled as a 3B result.

The current question is already owned at the right abstraction. EMBER-02
requires a measured full-shape 3B cost model covering bytes moved, optimizer
state, active tensors, FLOPs/token, wall time/token, power, disk traffic,
checkpoint cost, and recovery time before launch. Issue #707 owns the current
optimizer-state and memory-traffic hypothesis, including Muon deletion,
precision and mechanism variants, update survival, transition equivalence, and
matched quality attribution on an admissible clean-genesis 3B-or-larger subject.

Executing #702's frozen 2.2B fixture would violate the active model floor.
Changing it to a current 3B architecture would change the checkpoint identity,
parameter groups, optimizer mechanism, transfer inventory, schedule, resource
envelope, and predicates. This is architectural supersession, not evidence that
the historical attribution hypothesis wins or loses.

## Lossless obligation transfer

The following unique #702 obligations transfer to canonical issue #707 and the
version-controlled EMBER-02 contract:

- derive every timing and byte counter from the exact admitted current 3B
  checkpoint, source bytes, config, optimizer groups, non-null gradients,
  tokenizer/data lineage, and schedule; reject reference constants or
  total-numel surrogates that do not match the live subject;
- instrument the actual production consumer, never a sibling or copied
  reimplementation, with exclusive fwd, loss, bwd, transfer, storage, Muon,
  AdamW, and mechanism-specific optimizer spans sufficient to evaluate the
  frozen branch predicates without aggregating distinct causes;
- use at least two warmup and ten active steps per block, identical forked
  state and data positions, counterbalanced profiled/unprofiled AB/BA arms,
  profiler-overhead and five-percent wall-closure gates, and deterministic
  bootstrap confidence intervals;
- bind exact D2H/H2D and persistent-storage bytes, pageable versus pinned
  calibration, host memory and commit, page faults, disk IO, CPU utilization
  and affinity, GPU power/utilization/VRAM, effective threads, and current
  interval-wide single-tenant evidence; unavailable decisive measurements
  refuse rather than becoming disclosed skips;
- retain the non-post-hoc verdict grammar and distinguish staging, unavoidable
  host movement, Muon/NS work, other Muon work, AdamW work, file-backed traffic,
  and GPU compute before routing a cure;
- require the selected current-native cure to run as a matched A/B on the same
  admitted subject and satisfy the durable major-reduction bar: targeted
  component reduction, end-to-end throughput lower bound, matched loss,
  update-survival, stability, and the same resource envelope;
- preserve every negative, inconclusive, deletion, rollback, and source-drift
  result in the evidence graph without promoting historical 434M/2.2B evidence
  across scale or mechanism;
- route any current measurement exclusively through Ember Lab, the governed
  runner, and the current custody and receipt spine. No historical daemon,
  cbase launcher, parallel lease, or parallel receipt authority may be revived.

Issue #707 owns the optimizer hypothesis, variants, controls, sequencing, and
claim grammar. EMBER-02 owns the admissible 3B birth and full-shape cost-model
measurement. #702 owned only the retired scale-specific attribution vehicle; no
unique obligation remains after this transfer.

The transfer is not merely asserted by this document. The exact residual
clauses above were posted append-only to canonical #707 and accepted on its
live public thread before #702 closure:
<https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/707#issuecomment-5220742929>.
That comment binds this PR head, preserves the no-result boundary, and names
#707 plus EMBER-02 as the current owners. The prior #702
`KEEP_OPEN_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` comments remain truthful for the retired 2.2B
vehicle; this ruling does not reinterpret them as completed evidence.
