# Issue #518 activity-transcript conservation

Status: `SUPERSEDED_NOT_PLANNED`

## Ruling

Issue #518's standalone historical activity-transcript carrier is superseded
by canonical EMBER-03 plus the single current Ember Lab operational/event
authority. PR #528 is partial implementation evidence for its implemented
sources only. It does not complete the missing action classes, canonical-ledger
binding, uniform anatomy, or native fresh-reader gate.

## Lossless current-owner transfer

Canonical owners:
[EMBER-03/#1117](https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/1117) and the
current Ember Lab operational contract under
[#898](https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/898).

Accepted transfers:

- https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/1117#issuecomment-5224505748
- https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/898#issuecomment-5224505950

The accepted transfer preserves:

1. **Every distinct organism class.** Tool run, file write, lane/agent spawn,
   scheduler tick, governed training/evaluation launch and phase writes,
   receipt-bank landing, checkpoint/completion, and final run completion remain
   separate required classes. #528 proves only the shape for its implemented
   receipt/board/watchdog/outage sources, with current goal-session support.
   Missing tool/write/spawn/tick and training/evaluation phase producers remain
   unexecuted.
2. **Uniform transcript anatomy.** Each class remains a first-class reload-safe
   conversation block at its temporal position, with verb/object/count-or-size
   summary, expandable first-N detail and fold marker, closed
   model-tier/lane/run-id class tags, and exact
   source/executable/run/job/lease/process/telemetry/event identity. #528 does
   not prove uniform anatomy across all required classes.
3. **Bottom-status boundary.** Only live in-flight state remains on the bottom
   status line. Completed activity belongs in the transcript and must never
   exist only in bottom chrome. The landed removal of the activity ticker from
   the status line is implementation evidence, not universal closure.
4. **Native fresh-reader experience gate.** Acceptance uses the current
   installed Ember CLI side-by-side with the field exemplar at the operator's
   half-screen viewport, exercises live resize and reflow, requires rapid
   identification of tool runs, spawns, and writes, scores at least 8, and
   attaches a machine-minted receipt. #528's PTY/xterm-headless evidence and
   predicted score do not satisfy the native fresh-reader gate.
5. **Operator semantics.** Activity remains the visible agentic transcript, not
   decorative telemetry. Preserve the issue's operator quote and category
   semantics without inferring completion from prose.
6. **Canonical ledger custody.** Pin the canonical Ember Lab
   activity/operational ledger and refuse or loudly disclose non-canonical
   cwd-local roots. The historical worktree-local divergent ledger is a
   negative finding, not authority. No divergent ledger may be admitted.
7. **Identity, ordering, and negatives.** Preserve temporal ordering,
   deduplication, exact source/run/lease/job/process bindings, and malformed,
   stale, replayed, foreign, missing, deletion, interruption, failure, and
   rollback negatives. Current dispatch/lease/Job Object/operational receipts
   remain receipt-before-abort and reject foreign or legacy launchers.

Accepted canonical substrate remains the #1117 transfers for installed graph
and geometry evidence (#894), live telemetry (#140), machine-minted operator
sessions (#154), and continuation/journal behavior (#200), plus the current
Ember Lab operational obligations conserved through #898/#627. Those owners
preserve the evidence law; they do not execute the missing #518 producers or
native experience gate.
