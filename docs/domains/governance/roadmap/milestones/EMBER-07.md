# EMBER-07 — Seven-billion-parameter hard rung

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.OUTCOME.001 -->
Grow the autonomous 3B Ember organism into a sufficiently trained 7B-class clean-genesis unified model while preserving every required capability and making the Verified Expert Accretion advantage over matched controls larger than it was at the 3B hard rung.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.OUTCOME.002 -->
The rung proves meaningful learned scale, not simply that seven billion parameters can be allocated or stored.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.START.001 -->
- EMBER-05 established a deletion-testable 3B accretion advantage and froze the 3B active-cost reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.START.002 -->
- EMBER-06 established that the 3B organism can materially participate in its own research without external cognitive life support.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.START.003 -->
- The next scientific question is whether useful accreted capacity, routing, transfer, and native reasoning improve as total scale passes 7B while active work remains bounded.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.START.004 -->
- All architecture, data, optimizer, expert, memory, world-model, and benchmark identities must descend from the clean 3B manifest.

## Required work

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.001 -->
1. Pre-register the exact 7B total-capacity target, expert families, active set, data and experience budget, training FLOPs, stopping rule, evaluation, controls, and expected widening signature before growth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.002 -->
2. Grow through differentiated verified experience, not predetermined parameter scheduling. Train one candidate expert family at a time.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.003 -->
3. Keep one shared unified core and native text-image-audio interface; new capacity cannot become disconnected modality models.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.004 -->
4. Preserve and exercise structured tools, memory hierarchy, world models, dreaming, and autonomous research behavior during the rung rather than testing them only afterward.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.005 -->
5. Run dense restart, schedule growth, and ungated expert accumulation at matched tokens/FLOPs, data access, tuning allowance, and evaluation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.006 -->
6. Evaluate frozen language, mathematics, code, image, audio, native reasoning, tools, agents, computer use, research, memory, and interactive-world tasks against the 3B checkpoint, matched controls, and frozen 7B open-model references.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.007 -->
7. Require deletion-sensitive attribution for promoted experts, routes, memory/consolidation changes, and claimed cross-domain gains.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.008 -->
8. Measure peak GPU memory, active parameters, FLOPs/token, wall time/token, energy/token, bytes moved, checkpoint cost, and total elapsed creation cost on the production path.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.009 -->
9. Maintain peak device memory at or below 23.5 GiB and active trainable capacity at or below 4B per episode.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.010 -->
10. Keep the upper confidence bound of matched per-token FLOPs, energy, and wall time at or below 1.25 times the frozen 3B reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.011 -->
11. Demonstrate a statistically larger accretion-versus-control advantage than EMBER-05, not merely a positive 7B gap.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.WORK.012 -->
12. Have Ember participate materially in hypothesis selection, experiment operation, diagnosis, and evidence interpretation under the EMBER-06 assistance boundary.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Counting a 7B allocation, short continuation, or partially trained expert bank as the rung.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Freezing or omitting image, audio, native reasoning, tools, memory, world models, or autonomous research because scale work is expensive.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Changing controls, data, stopping, or tuning asymmetrically.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Increasing active compute with total capacity and calling the cost nearly constant because GPU memory still fits.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Reporting average cost while hiding tail, recovery, host, disk, or checkpoint cost.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Using an external 7B model for training signal or as a hidden task solver.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Claiming widening without the frozen 3B effect and uncertainty in the same analysis.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.001 -->
One current 7B rung receipt must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.002 -->
1. At least 7,000,000,000 unique total parameters with exact active/trainable/served/trained counts and clean descent from the 3B genesis.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.003 -->
2. The frozen sufficient-training or statistical stopping rule completed.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.004 -->
3. Text, image, audio, native reasoning, structured tools, memory, world-model, and autonomous-research capabilities all meet their frozen rung floors and show no material regression from 3B.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.005 -->
4. Verified Expert Accretion beats all three matched controls and its advantage is statistically larger than at 3B.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.006 -->
5. Responsible deletion erases each attributed gain and irrelevant deletion does not.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.007 -->
6. Peak GPU memory is at most 23.5 GiB, active trainable capacity at most 4B, and matched per-token cost upper bounds at most 1.25 times the 3B reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.008 -->
7. The exact checkpoint, experts, router, memory, backend, benchmarks, and receipts reload through one identity manifest.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.CERT.009 -->
8. Ember's own research contribution remains material and assistance boundaries remain satisfied.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-07.REOPEN.001 -->
A negative 7B result updates the tested scale, routing, expert, data, precision, or interaction regime while EMBER-07 remains active. It does not make 7B optional or authorize a smaller destination. If later evidence invalidates the 3B reference or shows a hidden active-cost increase, both EMBER-05 and EMBER-07 reopen.
