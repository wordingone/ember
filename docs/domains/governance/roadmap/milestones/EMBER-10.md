# EMBER-10 — General sovereign local AI laboratory

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.OUTCOME.001 -->
Prove that Ember did not merely discover a one-off way to create itself. The final Ember must substantially operate the local laboratory and create at least one materially different, useful, clean-genesis foundation intelligence of at least 3B parameters on the same 24GB GPU.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.OUTCOME.002 -->
The created intelligence is not an Ember expert, adapter, fine-tune, sparse extension, renamed checkpoint, or derivative of borrowed weights. It has its own identity manifest, architecture hypothesis, lineage, training, evaluation, and capability evidence.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.START.001 -->
- EMBER-09 proves a specific 30-35B Ember and its creation primitive, not yet general local foundation-model creation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.START.002 -->
- A general laboratory must support materially different architectures and objectives rather than only descendants optimized for Ember's existing routing and data representation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.START.003 -->
- Every created intelligence remains subject to clean genesis and the 3B minimum; generality does not authorize a local fine-tuning or distillation factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.START.004 -->
- Ember is now capable enough to act as resident researcher, engineer, experimenter, and operator, while executable reality remains the verification authority.

## Required work

### Define material difference before creation

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.001 -->
1. Select a consequential intelligence objective that is not satisfied by adding an Ember expert or changing its system prompt.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.002 -->
2. Pre-register why the candidate is materially different in causal computation, update mechanism, memory/state representation, routing/organization, objective, or modality interaction.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.003 -->
3. Predict at least one behavior, scaling signature, failure mode, or deletion signature that distinguishes the new architecture from Ember.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.004 -->
4. Freeze a matched Ember-derived control to prevent cosmetic differences from passing.

### Ember-operated creation

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.005 -->
5. Have Ember perform the majority of research decomposition, literature/source inspection, architecture design, corpus and tokenizer planning, implementation, experiment control, monitoring, diagnosis, evidence interpretation, and next-action selection.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.006 -->
6. Keep the outside development role to invariant protection, consequential authority, independent verification, and intervention only when a recorded boundary requires it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.007 -->
7. Use the owned laboratory stack while allowing architecture-specific owned extensions. No external model may design, generate data for, filter, judge, or operate the created intelligence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.008 -->
8. Build a distinct clean identity manifest and genesis record. No Ember weight, expert, adapter, memory, output, or hidden state may seed the new model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.009 -->
9. Train at least 3,000,000,000 parameters sufficiently on the single 24GB GPU. Non-learning fixtures may be smaller; the created intelligence may not.

### Prove useful generality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.010 -->
10. Evaluate the new model on frozen tasks suited to its objective plus broad language, native reasoning, tool, and relevant modality floors.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.011 -->
11. Compare against its pre-registered Ember-derived control, dense or standard control, and frozen external references where appropriate.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.012 -->
12. Run deletion or intervention tests on the feature that makes the architecture materially different.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.013 -->
13. Demonstrate at least one useful capability or efficiency result attributable to the distinct architecture and not reproduced by the cosmetic control.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.WORK.014 -->
14. Recreate the laboratory run from public code, receipted data, and exact manifests without an outside AI development agent or a hosted model supplying missing cognition.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Calling a new expert bank, adapter, prompt, memory collection, quantization, or router setting a materially different intelligence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Seeding from Ember or borrowed weights and declaring a new genesis.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Letting Ember's outputs become training data, teacher labels, filters, rewards, or judges for the new model. Ember may operate deterministic tools but cannot enter the target's learning signal.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Choosing material difference after results are visible.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Creating a sub-3B demonstration model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Counting architecture code, a forward pass, or a short smoke schedule as creation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Treating one specialized task gain as a foundation intelligence without broad competence and native reasoning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Requiring outside AI development agents, a subscription, or a hosted API to complete normal laboratory operation.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.001 -->
One independent general-laboratory certificate must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.002 -->
1. A separate clean genesis and at least 3B unique parameters with no Ember or external-model training-signal contribution.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.003 -->
2. A preregistered materially different architecture and predicted distinguishing signatures.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.004 -->
3. Sufficient training, exact custody, and reproducible creation on the single 24GB GPU.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.005 -->
4. Broad useful capability including native reasoning and the modalities/tools required by its declared objective.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.006 -->
5. A capability, efficiency, or generalization result that the matched cosmetic/Ember-derived control does not reach.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.007 -->
6. Deletion or intervention evidence linking the result to the materially different mechanism.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.008 -->
7. Ember performed the majority of laboratory cognition and operation within the assistance boundary.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.CERT.009 -->
8. Clean recreation succeeds without hosted cognition, secret state, or operator life support.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-10.REOPEN.001 -->
A failed candidate remains evidence about that architecture and laboratory process. It does not weaken the requirement that the lab create a materially different intelligence, nor does it force deletion of the tested family. If later review finds hidden Ember signal, cosmetic difference, inadequate training, or external cognitive dependence, EMBER-10 reopens.
