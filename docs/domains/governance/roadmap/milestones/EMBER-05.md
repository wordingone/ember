# EMBER-05 — Three-billion-parameter Verified Expert Accretion rung

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.OUTCOME.001 -->
Prove the headline causal mechanism at the first hard rung: repeated locally verified experience grows differentiated, useful, attributable capacity from the 3B Ember while only the required expert set is active, and the result beats matched dense restart, schedule growth, and ungated expert accumulation.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.OUTCOME.002 -->
This goal converts the 3B foundation birth into the first verified self-improving organism. It trains one candidate expert family at a time and preserves every negative or interaction result in the evidence graph.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.START.001 -->
- Structural widening alone is not learning. Historical expert twins remained identical and added no usable capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.START.002 -->
- Multiple loop families moved adapters, policies, routers, code artifacts, or narrow metrics, but no end-to-end cycle proved meaningful improvement of the owned foundation model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.START.003 -->
- Tool-R0 and progressive expert-expansion research provide outside evidence for generator/solver co-evolution and efficient expansion, but their weights and outputs cannot enter Ember.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.START.004 -->
- The 3B Ember now has a unified multimodal body, verified memory/world-model substrates, and typed tools, making locally grounded experience possible.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.START.005 -->
- Exact expert-to-core consolidation remains an empirical variable.

## Required work

### Generator, solver, verifier

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.001 -->
1. Run a local generator that creates frontier tasks across code, mathematics, SQL, files, browser/UI, images, audio, research, and interactive worlds from clean human/deterministic sources and Ember's own receipted state.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.002 -->
2. Have the exact Ember solver reason and act through typed tools. Record observations, intermediate actions where externally observable, tool results, resource use, and final outcomes.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.003 -->
3. Admit an episode to learning only when an independent deterministic or grounded verifier can decide the relevant property. Model self-grading and borrowed-model judgment are forbidden.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.004 -->
4. Preserve failed, ambiguous, and unverifiable episodes without training on them as positive signal.

### Differentiated growth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.005 -->
5. Create or update one candidate expert family at a time. Use asymmetric initialization, partial reinitialization, data specialization, or other mechanisms that produce measurable differentiation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.006 -->
6. Route at task or episode level so only the required expert set and shared core are active. Measure total, active, trainable, loaded, and actually updated parameters.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.007 -->
7. Give vision, audio, reasoning, tool, memory/world-model, and other domains distinct expert hypotheses where evidence supports separation, while testing cross-domain composition and transfer.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.008 -->
8. Compare modular retention, routing, adapter buffers, expert-to-core consolidation, dual representation, and migration based on capability, transfer, interference, cost, plasticity, and recoverability.

### Matched causal program

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.009 -->
9. Freeze equal tokens, effective clean tokens, training FLOPs, data access, initialization evidence, evaluation, stopping, and tuning allowance for:
   - Verified Expert Accretion;
   - dense restart;
   - predetermined schedule growth; and
   - ungated expert accumulation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.010 -->
10. Measure held-out text, image, audio, native reasoning, tools, memory, world-model, and cross-domain composition after repeated cycles.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.011 -->
11. Delete or roll back each promoted expert, route, memory update, and consolidation object separately. The claimed gain must collapse in the responsible deletion and survive irrelevant deletions.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.012 -->
12. Establish at least two ordered 3B-class accumulation points showing that the advantage over matched controls increases rather than shrinks as verified capacity grows.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.013 -->
13. Keep peak GPU memory within 24GB and report active per-episode FLOPs, wall time, energy, bytes moved, and updated parameter count.

### 2026-07-16 constant-active causal-measurement tightening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.014 -->
This amendment tightens EMBER-05 without weakening its Outcome or Completion certificate, and it does not block EMBER-02.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.015 -->
14. At matched, constant active-parameter and episode-compute budgets, measure capability as total differentiated capacity accrues. Report retained old-domain depth, new-domain breadth, cross-domain composition, and native reasoning depth at every ordered accumulation point.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.016 -->
15. Bind each point to router load and entropy plus shared-trunk parameter and representation drift, so apparent gains can be distinguished from route collapse, shared-core overwrite, or increased active computation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.017 -->
16. Run responsible expert deletion attribution and the already-required matched dense-restart, predetermined-schedule-growth, and ungated-accumulation controls under the same frozen budgets.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.WORK.018 -->
17. Issue exactly one causal verdict:
   - `SUPPORT`: retained depth, acquired breadth, cross-domain composition, and native reasoning depth improve with deletion-attributable capacity at constant active cost, while matched controls do not explain the gain;
   - `BREADTH_ONLY`: new domains are added without the required retained or deeper capability; this is useful capacity growth but is not frontier-rivaling success; or
   - `REFUTE_REOPEN`: attribution, retention, reasoning depth, constant-active scaling, or matched-control separation fails, reopening EMBER-05 under the Failure and reopening rule.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Exact expert copying or parameter allocation without differentiated learning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Training multiple candidate expert families concurrently and selecting after unregistered comparison.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Letting a model, including Ember, serve as the only verifier of its own promotion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Counting more total parameters, lower training loss alone, or one narrow task gain as usable capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Omitting dense restart, schedule growth, or ungated expert accumulation because they are expensive or weak.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Tuning only the favored arm or changing data/stopping after results are visible.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Treating tool-harness competence as native reasoning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Erasing a mechanism or research family because one isolated regime is negative.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.001 -->
One frozen multi-cycle campaign must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.002 -->
1. At least three consecutive verified-experience cycles produce promoted neural updates or experts in the exact 3B lineage.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.003 -->
2. Promoted experts are functionally and numerically differentiated from their source and from one another.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.004 -->
3. Held-out capability improves across multiple domains, including native reasoning and at least one image and one audio task, with no required-domain material regression.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.005 -->
4. Responsible deletion or rollback erases each attributed gain; irrelevant deletion does not.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.006 -->
5. Verified Expert Accretion beats dense restart, schedule growth, and ungated expert accumulation at matched tokens/FLOPs under the frozen analysis.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.007 -->
6. Its advantage increases across at least two ordered accumulation points.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.008 -->
7. Active expert set, GPU memory, per-token cost, and storage are measured and consistent with the route to 7B and 30-35B total capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.009 -->
8. All learning signal is clean-genesis and independently verified.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.CERT.010 -->
9. The final checkpoint, router, experts, memory, backend, and benchmark receipts resolve through one identity manifest and reload exactly.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-05.REOPEN.001 -->
Negative evidence narrows the tested initialization, routing, domain, ordering, consolidation, precision, or scale regime. It does not delete expert growth, dense controls, or the broader goal. If later scaling shows the apparent advantage shrinking, losing deletion attribution, or regressing a modality, EMBER-05 reopens and later rung claims stop.
