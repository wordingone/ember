# EMBER-08 — Fifteen-billion-parameter hard rung

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.OUTCOME.001 -->
Grow the verified 7B organism into a sufficiently trained 15B-class clean-genesis unified model while preserving all required capabilities, the single-GPU active-cost envelope, autonomous research competence, and a Verified Expert Accretion advantage that widens again relative to 7B.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.OUTCOME.002 -->
The rung is the decisive mid-scale test that sparse total capacity is becoming useful rather than accumulating dormant parameters.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.START.001 -->
- The 7B rung supplied a second scale point with a wider accretion advantage and bounded active cost.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.START.002 -->
- Fifteen billion total parameters materially increases routing, storage, interference, retrieval, and expert-composition pressure while still preceding the final 30-35B target.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.START.003 -->
- Any weakness hidden at 15B is likely to become prohibitive above 27B; hardest-core-first measurement applies.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.START.004 -->
- The clean 3B-to-7B lineage, controls, benchmark registry, and active-cost reference remain binding.

## Required work

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.001 -->
1. Freeze the exact 15B architecture state, expert composition, active set, verified-experience budget, data, controls, stopping, benchmark suite, and expected cross-rung effect before training.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.002 -->
2. Accrete differentiated experts one candidate family at a time, using Ember's own research competence to select experiments while independent verification gates learning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.003 -->
3. Test expert composition across text, image, audio, native reasoning, tools, memory, world models, and research rather than measuring isolated specialist scores.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.004 -->
4. Measure router quality, expert utilization, collision, dead capacity, transfer, interference, retrieval latency, storage traffic, and consolidation behavior.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.005 -->
5. Run matched dense restart, schedule growth, and ungated expert accumulation at equal tokens/FLOPs, data access, tuning allowance, and stopping.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.006 -->
6. Re-run the frozen broad external benchmark and interactive-world suite against 3B, 7B, frozen 15B-class references where available, and all matched controls.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.007 -->
7. Apply expert, route, memory, world-model, and consolidation deletion tests to every promoted claim.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.008 -->
8. Preserve peak GPU memory at or below 23.5 GiB, active trainable capacity at or below 4B, and the upper confidence bound of matched per-token FLOPs, energy, and wall time at or below 1.25 times the 3B reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.009 -->
9. Measure full elapsed creation cost separately from active episode cost; do not hide cumulative expert training.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.010 -->
10. Require the accretion-versus-control effect to exceed the frozen 7B effect with preregistered uncertainty.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.011 -->
11. Require native reasoning to improve or remain within the frozen non-regression bound on unseen multi-step multimodal and tool tasks, with harness/search ablations.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.WORK.012 -->
12. Require Ember to diagnose and correct at least one real 15B-scale systems or learning failure without external cognitive rescue.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Treating unused or unreachable experts as effective 15B capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Reporting total parameters without trained-token and utilization distributions.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Allowing a modality, reasoning, tool, memory, or world-model regression to be averaged away by text gains.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Raising active parameters or per-token cost above the envelope and redefining “nearly constant.”
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Omitting cumulative creation cost or comparing it with only one dense-control run while accretion receives repeated tuning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Reusing 7B results as 15B evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Accepting a smaller or partially trained checkpoint because full 15B is slow.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.001 -->
One current 15B rung receipt must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.002 -->
1. At least 15,000,000,000 unique total parameters, exact active/trainable/served/trained counts, and clean descent from the 7B lineage.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.003 -->
2. Sufficient training under the frozen stopping rule and meaningful utilization of promoted capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.004 -->
3. No required text, image, audio, native reasoning, tool, memory, world-model, or autonomous-research capability materially regresses.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.005 -->
4. Verified Expert Accretion beats all matched controls and its advantage is statistically larger than at 7B.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.006 -->
5. Deletion and rollback causally bind every promoted capability to the responsible state.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.007 -->
6. Peak GPU memory, active capacity, and matched per-token cost remain inside the unchanged envelope.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.008 -->
7. Full elapsed creation cost and cumulative expert cost are disclosed and compatible with the >27B route.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.CERT.009 -->
8. The checkpoint and organism reload exactly through the identity spine and Ember remains capable of operating its research laboratory.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-08.REOPEN.001 -->
Negative evidence narrows the tested 15B design or interaction while the rung remains active. It does not permit skipping directly to >27B or erasing the failed mechanism. If later final-scale work reveals a 15B regression, utilization illusion, or mispriced cost, EMBER-08 reopens.
