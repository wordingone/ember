# Owned architecture and mechanism research contract

This contract is subordinate to GOAL.md. It specifies the research space; it
does not authorize a smaller launch vehicle or a published-family backbone.

## Unified decoder contract

Every admissible model rung uses one owned decoder. Raw image patches and audio
frames project directly into decoder tokens, soft tokens splice into the shared
sequence, multimodal spans receive the required attention semantics, and rotary
position structure supports two-dimensional perception. Text, image, audio,
reasoning, and structured tool use are native at the first 3B birth.

Published architecture studies can motivate properties. Llama, Gemma, Qwen, or
any other published family cannot be Ember's backbone, copied implementation,
weight seed, teacher, judge, or hidden capability provider.

## Sparse differentiated capacity

The destination combines a shared core with independently trainable expert
banks and task-level routing. Only episode-required experts are active.
Vision, audio, reasoning, tool, memory, and other domains may have distinct
experts inside the same decoder.

Exact-duplicate twins do not count as growth. Upcycling is asymmetric or
partially reinitialized, trained one candidate expert family at a time, and
promoted only after transfer, persistence, non-regression, and deletion tests.

Expert-to-core consolidation frequency, retained modularity, routing topology,
precision, order, and merge semantics are open technical variables. They are
not fixed by convenience or an invented rarity rule.

## Conserved mechanism portfolio

The research graph includes, without being limited to:

- quantization-aware training, ternary and BitNet-style numerics, and owned
  low-bit storage/export;
- sub-quadratic attention and state, MTP, SDEK/adaptation control, RLM, iGRPO,
  and verified generator/solver co-evolution;
- optimizer and update-computation work, custom kernels, compiler work, SDPA
  replacement, memory/residency, recomputation, and checkpoint efficiency;
- systematic transformation of inference optimizations into training
  optimizations;
- historical and modern research journals, laboratory notebooks, letters,
  and documented self-improvement processes across scientific, technical,
  artistic, and professional fields, tested for transferable process value
  rather than copied as prose;
- episodic memory, temporary adapters or fast weights, replay consolidation,
  dream/sleep mechanisms, durable parametric burns, routers, and world models.

A mechanism can be inactive in the current configuration. Negative evidence
records the exact regime. No agent status can kill, retire, exclude, indefinitely
park, delete, or force a named successor for a research family. Composition and
later regime tests remain available.

## Scale and experiment boundary

No new neural candidate, proxy, pilot, control, evaluation network, or served
research network may be below 3,000,000,000 total parameters. Existing smaller
artifacts are read-only historical evidence. Deterministic non-neural unit
fixtures remain allowed when they cannot receive model or capability credit.

Every executable candidate declares total, trainable, and active parameters;
all five native capabilities; exact goal_id and next executed outcome; clean
lineage; sufficient-training plan; equal-budget controls; and rollback.
