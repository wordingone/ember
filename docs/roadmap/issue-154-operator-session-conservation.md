# Issue #154 operator-session conservation

Status: `SUPERSEDED_CURRENT_EMBER_03`

## Ruling

Issue #154 predates the current Ember Lab and EMBER-03 authority surfaces. Its
old maintainer-session, standing-loop, brain-server, and surface-port vehicles
must not be revived as a second operator, launcher, daemon, or receipt path.

The issue's unique contract is still current: a human must be able to operate
Ember through the visible `ember-cli` surface, and autonomy claims must derive
from machine-minted, replayable operator-session evidence. Canonical EMBER-03
has accepted that complete contract. The historical tracker can therefore close
as superseded without claiming that a qualifying session or R0 result exists.

## Historical evidence boundary

The 2026-07-05 progress comment records a real prompt injection and echo through
the historical pipe, followed by a non-completing turn. It was blocked by the
then-current repository-root defect and did not satisfy the acceptance bar.

The pinned 2026-07-06 R0 preregistration explicitly excludes sessions 1–5 and
forbids retroactive completion from a first render, a handwritten receipt, an
error, a wedge, or a non-responsive answer. Those negative facts remain
historical evidence; this ruling does not convert them into a completed session.

## Lossless current-owner transfer

Canonical owner: [EMBER-03/#1117](https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/1117).

Accepted transfer: https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/1117#issuecomment-5222105526

The accepted transfer preserves:

1. visible same-surface input and reply through current `ember-cli`, never a
   hidden headless bypass;
2. a machine-minted, reload-safe chain binding current source, executable,
   session and launch identity, connection, visible prompt injection, terminal
   result, prompt, reply, next action, and pixel frame;
3. first-session goal injection through Ember's own surface, followed by
   observation and recorded correction;
4. a durable prompt → observe → correct operator leg until that interaction is
   optional, not a second standing-loop authority;
5. intervention counts derived from admitted session receipts for the R-rung
   autonomy ladder, never inferred from prose or an idle interface;
6. responsive rendered success plus same-turn operator judgment for each
   completed session, with errors, wedges, zombies, flailing answers, and
   missing frames retained as negative evidence;
7. the frozen R0 bar of at least three completed sessions, at least two prompt
   classes, at least two separate current-public-build cockpit launches, zero
   wedge or zombie inside the counted sessions, citation of every counted
   receipt, and disclosure of non-counted wedges on the same build;
8. malformed, foreign-session, interruption, deletion, rollback, and negative
   evidence, with no inference to training, checkpoint quality, capability,
   sufficient pretraining, or milestone completion.

## Architecture and claim boundary

Current Ember Lab owned-server supervision, CLI input and rendering, goal mode,
activity journal, and receipt/custody paths remain the sole authority. This
ruling creates no second daemon, launcher, operator loop, session schema,
receipt family, or hidden automation path.

`NO_NEW_PARALLEL_AUTHORITY`

No qualifying operator session, R0 result, training, checkpoint, model
capability, sufficient-pretraining, or milestone-completion claim is made.
