# EMBER-04 — Grounded memory, world model, and dreaming

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.OUTCOME.001 -->
Give the existing 3B Ember a causally verified cognitive state across episodes: working memory, episodic custody, temporary neural adaptation, durable parametric memory, predictive world models, cross-world action representations, and genuine replay/dream consolidation.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.OUTCOME.002 -->
This goal may update the existing qualifying 3B network and add state attached to it. It may not instantiate a separate smaller network or count symbolic storage as learned memory.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.001 -->
- Current frontier agents depend heavily on files, search, retrieval, compaction, and external state. Ember's local ownership permits real parametric updates but does not make every remembered item suitable for weights.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.002 -->
- The recovered design treats KV/context as perception window and working memory; external custody as episodic memory; adapters or fast weights as temporary neural buffers; and experts/shared core as possible durable memory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.003 -->
- Exact expert retention, routing, adapter merging, and core-consolidation frequency remain empirical questions. “Rare” is not an operator requirement.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.004 -->
- Historical dream-loop naming conflated symbolic planning and receipts with neural replay and consolidation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.005 -->
- World-model intent covered predictive latent dynamics, a shared learned model across worlds, deterministic world adapters, primitive actions, learned future proposals, grounded verification, and benchmark/data roles. Only interface seams currently exist.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.START.006 -->
- Historical and modern journals, experiment logs, letters, and profession-specific trajectories were intended to expose how humans revise methods and models, but no complete process-trajectory learning system exists.

## Required work

### Memory hierarchy

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.001 -->
1. Treat the current multimodal context and KV state as perception plus working memory. Measure what is retained, overwritten, compressed, retrieved, and causally used.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.002 -->
2. Store verified experiences in append-only episodic custody with task, observation, action, tool, verifier, outcome, uncertainty, model identity, and privacy/retention metadata.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.003 -->
3. Implement temporary neural adaptation through full-model-compatible fast weights, LoRA-like adapters, or equivalent buffers attached to the 3B identity. They are not separate models and cannot incorporate external-model signal.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.004 -->
4. Research whether durable capabilities remain modular, route through experts, consolidate into shared core, occupy both, or migrate adaptively. Compare capability, transfer, interference, plasticity, storage, active cost, recoverability, and deletion attribution.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.005 -->
5. Make memory retrieval and update visible in ember-cli without exposing private content by default.

### Learned world model

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.006 -->
6. Implement learned predictive and counterfactual state dynamics inside the owned model rather than calling state-diff plumbing a world model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.007 -->
7. Use one transferable learned substrate across code, terminal, browser/UI, games, CAD, research environments, images, audio, and other worlds, with thin deterministic world adapters where needed.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.008 -->
8. Compile heterogeneous actions into a shared primitive grammar such as emit-token, emit-scalar, emit-pointer, commit, and stop; adapters translate primitives into world-native calls.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.009 -->
9. Let learned dynamics propose futures and actions while executable reality, deterministic tools, and environment state remain truth authorities.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.010 -->
10. Measure cross-world transfer, counterfactual calibration, planning value, and deletion-sensitive contribution on frozen tasks.

### Dreaming and human update trajectories

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.011 -->
11. Convert verified episodic experience and human process trajectories from journals and fields into replayable sequences of prior belief or method, question, attempt, observation, error, revision, preservation, and later transfer.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.012 -->
12. Implement sleep/dream cycles that replay, simulate alternatives, compress, and consolidate into identifiable neural state.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.013 -->
13. Compare awake-only learning, replay without update, update without counterfactuals, and full dream consolidation at matched experience and compute.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.014 -->
14. Require improved held-out behavior, transfer, and retention plus rollback/deletion evidence. A generated plan, summary, receipt, or file rewrite is not a dream result.

### Native reasoning through state

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.015 -->
15. Test whether memory and world-model state improves unseen multi-step reasoning across text, mathematics, code, image, audio, tools, and interactive worlds.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.WORK.016 -->
16. Separate reasoning supplied by the base checkpoint, retrieved episodes, learned predictive state, deterministic search, and tool execution through matched removals and counterfactual tests.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Calling files, vector retrieval, a database, context compaction, or prompt injection “neural memory.”
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Calling state diffs, replay logs, action codecs, or environment snapshots a learned world model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Calling a symbolic plan or synthetic transcript a dream because it is produced during idle time.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Merging adapters or experts into the core on a fixed schedule without evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Keeping every experience forever or uploading private memory to a central service.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Using an external model to summarize, label, score, choose, or consolidate memories.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Crediting search or tool execution as model reasoning without causal separation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Creating a small auxiliary neural memory or world model below 3B.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.001 -->
The exact 3B Ember identity must pass:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.002 -->
1. Working-memory engagement tests showing controlled retention, use, eviction, and interruption recovery.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.003 -->
2. Episodic write/read/replay custody with privacy, provenance, verifier, and exact model binding.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.004 -->
3. A temporary neural adaptation that produces a held-out gain, survives reload, and disappears on removal.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.005 -->
4. An empirically selected or adaptive durable-memory policy whose transfer/interference/cost/recoverability evidence beats the named alternatives in its claimed regime.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.006 -->
5. Learned next-state and counterfactual prediction beyond deterministic adapters on at least three materially different worlds.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.007 -->
6. Cross-world transfer through a shared learned substrate and primitive actions, with grounded environment verification.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.008 -->
7. A dream/consolidation cycle that changes neural state, improves frozen held-out retention or transfer over matched controls, and reverses under deletion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.009 -->
8. Native reasoning improvements causally attributable to model state rather than retrieval, scripted search, or tool policy alone.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.CERT.010 -->
9. No required modality, reasoning, or tool capability materially regresses.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-04.REOPEN.001 -->
Negative results narrow the tested memory representation, consolidation rule, world, or ordering. They do not erase memory, dreaming, world models, or their possible interactions. If later expert growth breaks retention, transfer, privacy, or attribution, EMBER-04 reopens before the affected larger rung can clear.
