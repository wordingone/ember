# EMBER-06 — Autonomous AI-research bootstrap

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.OUTCOME.001 -->
Demonstrate that the 3B Ember organism is already a useful resident AI researcher: it can identify a consequential local foundation-model problem, form and discriminate hypotheses, implement the experiment through its tools, operate the laboratory, interpret grounded evidence, preserve uncertainty, and select the next action without outside AI development agents, a subscription, or an API supplying the load-bearing cognition.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.OUTCOME.002 -->
The bootstrap must clear within 180 active program days after EMBER-05. Pauses explicitly imposed by the operator do not consume active program days; ordinary difficulty, retries, and slow local compute do.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.START.001 -->
- The body, memory, world-model, dreaming, and Verified Expert Accretion mechanisms now provide the minimum substrate for grounded research action.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.START.002 -->
- Earlier Ember loops largely depended on external agents to choose questions, write implementations, interpret receipts, and decide what happened next.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.START.003 -->
- Infrastructure can make a weak model look capable. This goal must separate the 3B checkpoint's native reasoning and learned research policy from deterministic search, tools, templates, and externally authored decisions.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.START.004 -->
- The operator wants outside AI development agents, when available, to carry routine transitional testing—not to become permanent invisible researchers or require constant operator approvals.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.START.005 -->
- Scientific negative results remain useful evidence but cannot be converted into requirement erasure.

## Required work

### Autonomous research cycle

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.001 -->
1. Give Ember a high-level invariant objective and current executable evidence, not a prewritten hypothesis or implementation plan.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.002 -->
2. Require Ember to inspect the identity spine, research history, live resource measurements, and relevant human-authored literature or source code without consulting another model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.003 -->
3. Have Ember identify the dominant uncertainty, propose multiple causally distinct hypotheses or interactions, predict discriminating outcomes, and select the cheapest decisive experiment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.004 -->
4. Require exact preregistration of subject, data, controls, budgets, engagement, stopping, analysis, and decision grammar before observing claim-bearing results.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.005 -->
5. Have Ember implement or modify the experiment through typed tools, run tests, execute the governed measurement, monitor resources, recover failures, and preserve raw evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.006 -->
6. Have Ember independently recompute the decisive statistics or predicates, distinguish fact from inference, update the evidence graph, and choose the next experiment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.007 -->
7. Bind every cognitive and operational contribution to actor and source so externally authored rescue cannot be hidden inside an “autonomous” run.

### Breadth and repeated competence

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.008 -->
8. Complete at least three end-to-end research cycles on distinct surfaces chosen from architecture, optimizer/update computation, kernels/compiler, data efficiency, multimodal learning, memory/world models, routing/expert accretion, or evaluation methodology.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.009 -->
9. At least one cycle must originate a materially new Ember research direction, mechanism interaction, or measurement that was not supplied as a menu by an outside AI development agent or a retrieved template.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.010 -->
10. At least one cycle must handle a negative or inconclusive result without deleting the invariant goal, fabricating certainty, or asking the operator to choose a routine technical alternative.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.011 -->
11. At least one cycle must produce an independently verified useful result that changes the next executed experiment or materially improves the owned stack/model.

### Native reasoning and assistance ablations

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.012 -->
12. Evaluate unseen research problems requiring multi-step causal reasoning, experimental design, code comprehension, quantitative analysis, and tool selection.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.013 -->
13. Compare full Ember with tool execution but no retrieved plan, with retrieval but restricted action, with selected memory/world-model components deleted, and with deterministic automation alone.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.014 -->
14. Demonstrate that native model reasoning supplies capability that the harness, search, verifier, and scripts cannot reproduce.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.015 -->
15. Measure human interventions by type and consequence. Routine observation, permission calibration, and independent verification are allowed; load-bearing hypothesis, implementation, interpretation, or next-action selection is not.

### Burden transfer

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.016 -->
16. Make Ember monitor its own long jobs, diagnose bounded failures, preserve checkpoints, update its research state, and surface only material ambiguities or consequential operator choices.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.017 -->
17. Exercise multiple operator relationship modes and show that competence and honesty support reduced friction without silent authority expansion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.WORK.018 -->
18. Keep the operator as owner, collaborator, and final source of voluntary direction—not an on-call debugger or cognitive component.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Calling a scripted pipeline autonomous because it invokes the model between fixed steps.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Giving Ember a externally authored experiment and counting execution as research origination.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Using an outside AI development agent, another model, a subscription, or an API to fill a failed reasoning or implementation step.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Counting papers read, code written, tests passed, issues opened, or receipts emitted without a completed research decision.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Treating a verifier's binary output as the scientific interpretation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Requiring operator selection among routine technical alternatives that Ember can evaluate locally.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Hiding intervention after the run or redefining autonomy around whatever assistance occurred.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Creating a new smaller research model.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.001 -->
An independent evaluator must verify:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.002 -->
1. Three complete research-cycle receipts on distinct surfaces, each binding the 3B checkpoint, tools, memory, data, code, run, evidence, and decision.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.003 -->
2. At least one Ember-originated research direction or interaction that was not preselected externally.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.004 -->
3. At least one independently reproduced useful result that changes the next executed experiment or improves Ember's model/stack.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.005 -->
4. Correct handling of one negative or inconclusive result without scope deletion or false promotion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.006 -->
5. Native reasoning ablations showing the checkpoint contributes beyond automation, retrieval, search, tools, and verifiers.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.007 -->
6. No load-bearing outside AI development agents, subscription, API, or borrowed-model cognition in the accepted cycles.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.008 -->
7. Decreasing routine human intervention across the accepted sequence without hidden privilege expansion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.009 -->
8. Completion within 180 active program days, with pause accounting public and deterministic.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.CERT.010 -->
9. No required modality, reasoning, tool, memory, world-model, or prior accretion capability materially regresses.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-06.REOPEN.001 -->
Failed research cycles remain evidence about the tested capability and assistance boundary. Ember receives additional verified experience and another bounded attempt; the goal does not retreat to permanent external operation. If later rungs again require an outside AI development agent or another model for normal research operation, EMBER-06 reopens.
