# EMBER-03 — Body, tools, and operator relationship

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.OUTCOME.001 -->
Make ember-cli the real, continuously usable body of the exact 3B Ember born under EMBER-02. The operator must be able to converse with Ember, observe what model is acting, inspect and interrupt its work, delegate goals through typed tools, and choose an explicit relationship and permission posture whose actual behavior has been tested.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.OUTCOME.002 -->
This goal improves the body around the existing 3B network. It does not create a second model, use a smaller stand-in, or attribute harness intelligence to Ember.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.001 -->
- ember-cli contains substantial real implementation, but prior parity claims outran live validation and its persisted default selected Qwen.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.002 -->
- The original operator floor was the totality of avir-cli, plus Ember-specific improvements such as a goal mode rivaling or exceeding Codex and contemporary coding-agent software.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.003 -->
- User and assistant messages were visually conflated; model/backend identity was visible in one place but contradicted the product name and expectation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.004 -->
- Historical headless avir-cli substitution obscured whether ember-cli itself worked. Headless fallback cannot prove parity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.005 -->
- The operator has never had an evidenced, sustained conversation and tool session with a qualifying Ember-owned checkpoint.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.START.006 -->
- Operator relationships must be configurable, explicit, revocable, and allowed to mature through demonstrated trust rather than fixed to either permanent approval prompts or permanent bypass.

## Required work

### One body-to-brain path

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.001 -->
1. Make the EMBER-02 checkpoint the default owned backend and obtain its identity only from the verified manifest. No fallback may silently select Qwen or another model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.002 -->
2. Keep borrowed models available through explicit `reference` seats that display their external identity before every session and prevent their outputs entering Ember's learning loop.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.003 -->
3. Bind conversation, tools, memory, goal mode, training controls, benchmark actions, and observability to the same loaded checkpoint and backend process.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.004 -->
4. Make backend launch, liveness, restart, context, and failure states truthful. A body outage cannot be rendered as a model answer.

### avir-cli floor and interaction quality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.005 -->
5. Inventory avir-cli's complete behavior and port or surpass every applicable capability through ember-cli-native tests. Do not prove parity by invoking avir-cli headlessly.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.006 -->
6. Give operator messages a clear light-gray or equivalently unambiguous treatment, stable vertical spacing, readable tool/event grouping, and distinct model, system, and error presentation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.007 -->
7. Show the exact owned model ID, checkpoint hash prefix, total/active/trained parameters, clean-genesis status, context usage, active goal, tools, permissions, and current resource state without overwhelming the conversation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.008 -->
8. Support text, images, and audio as first-class user inputs and model outputs or predictions according to the checkpoint contract.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.009 -->
9. Expose structured tool calls, results, verification state, reversible changes, and model-versus-harness attribution.

### Goal mode and tools

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.010 -->
10. Implement persistent objectives, immutable-to-executor goal text, status-only transitions, event-driven continuation, user preemption, soft budget landing, repeated-impasse blocking, and proof-bearing completion audits.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.011 -->
11. Make goal mode rival or exceed contemporary Codex and contemporary coding-agent software on sustained work, recovery, interruption, context continuation, tool use, and truthful completion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.012 -->
12. Provide typed local tools for files, shell, code, SQL, browser/UI, images, audio, benchmarks, experiments, and laboratory control. Every tool declares reversible/irreversible effects and verification authority.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.013 -->
13. Distinguish what Ember reasoned and selected from what a deterministic harness merely executed or verified. Tool success cannot substitute for native reasoning.

### Dynamic operator relationship

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.014 -->
14. Implement explicit modes covering observe/advice, per-action approval, grouped approval, accept-edits, bounded automatic operation, and operator-defined scopes for filesystem, network, execution, training, spending, communication, deployment, and third parties.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.015 -->
15. Make every scope visible, revocable, and unable to expand silently. Ember may propose a mode change with evidence but cannot grant itself authority.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.016 -->
16. Accumulate domain-specific trust from demonstrated competence, honesty, recovery, and scope discipline. Trust evidence and operator acceptance—not convenience—permit reduced friction.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.017 -->
17. Sandbox ambiguous instructions, conflicting evidence, destructive actions, irreversible effects, resource overruns, third-party impact, mode transitions, revocation, rollback, and attempted scope escape.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.WORK.018 -->
18. Preserve the operator's final direction while requiring Ember to challenge questionable decisions with grounded evidence rather than passive agreement.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Using avir-cli headless mode to claim ember-cli parity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Rendering Qwen or any reference model inside a shell labeled Ember without constant reference identity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Treating a configured permission mode as evidence that behavior follows it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Counting a tool wrapper's policy, search, or scripted plan as native model reasoning.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Hiding errors, restarts, borrowed fallbacks, model changes, or learning events to produce a smoother demo.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Requiring the operator to approve every routine action because relationship testing was not completed.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Letting outside AI development agents become permanent surrogate operators.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Creating a smaller conversational model for UI development or fallback.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.001 -->
One recorded, replayable acceptance campaign must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.002 -->
1. Fresh launch defaults to the exact EMBER-02 checkpoint with no borrowed fallback.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.003 -->
2. The operator conducts sustained text, image, and audio interaction and at least one verified multi-tool goal through ember-cli itself.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.004 -->
3. The UI distinctly renders operator, Ember, tools, events, warnings, and failures and displays exact identity throughout.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.005 -->
4. avir-cli totality is covered by an executable parity matrix; every claimed improvement has a live test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.006 -->
5. Goal mode survives interruption, process restart, context continuation, a failed action, repeated impasse, user preemption, and a genuine proof-bearing completion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.007 -->
6. Permission modes and trust transitions pass behavioral sandbox scenarios, including denial and rollback after overreach.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.008 -->
7. Borrowed reference seats cannot write training data, memory updates, promotion signals, or hidden model state.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.009 -->
8. Model-only, harness-only, and combined capabilities are separately measured and disclosed.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.CERT.010 -->
9. The operator can interrupt, inspect, revoke, restore, and export the session without corrupting checkpoint or memory custody.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-03.REOPEN.001 -->
Any live discrepancy between declared identity, permission, model state, or actual behavior blocks only that body claim and remains visible until corrected. If a later body release silently changes the model, weakens operator scope, hides attribution, or loses avir-cli floor behavior, EMBER-03 reopens.
