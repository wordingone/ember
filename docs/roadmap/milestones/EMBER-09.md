# EMBER-09 — Owned model above twenty-seven billion parameters

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.OUTCOME.001 -->
Create the non-negotiable model destination: a sufficiently trained, clean-genesis, Ember-owned sparse unified decoder above 27B total parameters, initially targeted at 30-35B, on the single 24GB GPU. It must be natively text-image-audio, reason, use structured tools, operate its memory/world-model/laboratory, and perform competitively with the strongest frozen 27B/31B open-model references.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.OUTCOME.002 -->
A 30B parameter identity with roughly 4B capability is explicit failure.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.START.001 -->
- The 3B, 7B, and 15B hard rungs established a clean unified lineage, autonomous research competence, bounded active cost, and an accretion advantage that widened at each scale.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.START.002 -->
- The 15B rung exposed and resolved the mid-scale routing, utilization, interference, storage, and consolidation problems needed for final growth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.START.003 -->
- Frozen 27B/31B open models are external references only. Their weights, outputs, tool traces, scores-as-rewards, and judgments cannot shape Ember's model.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.START.004 -->
- The final claim requires external capability parity as well as parameter scale; local-only proxy metrics cannot establish competitiveness.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.START.005 -->
- Whole-stack ownership is a completion condition, not an aspiration after the model exists.

## Required work

### Final growth and active-cost law

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.001 -->
1. Freeze a target between 30B and 35B total unique parameters, its expert/core composition, active set, verified-experience budget, data, stopping, controls, benchmark suite, and predicted widening signature before the claim-bearing run.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.002 -->
2. Accrete differentiated capacity one candidate expert family at a time through the verified generator/solver/verifier loop.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.003 -->
3. Keep active trainable capacity at or below 4B per episode, peak device memory at or below 23.5 GiB, and the upper confidence bound of matched per-token FLOPs, energy, and wall time at or below 1.25 times the frozen 3B reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.004 -->
4. Disclose cumulative total creation time and energy separately. Nearly constant active cost does not mean total expert training is free.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.005 -->
5. Measure expert utilization, dead capacity, routing entropy and calibration, cross-expert composition, retrieval latency, storage traffic, core interference, and consolidation behavior.

### Full native stack

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.006 -->
6. Run the creation and inference path through Ember-owned implementations of the model architecture, training and inference runtime, optimizer/update computation, kernels, compiler-critical operations, attention/state, numeric formats, checkpointing, storage, routing, serving, and evaluation glue.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.007 -->
7. Name every remaining third-party deterministic dependency and prove it is replaceable infrastructure rather than hidden model cognition or a load-bearing borrowed creation primitive.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.008 -->
8. Reproduce the final checkpoint from clean genesis or receipted ancestors without private untracked code, data, state, or manual rescue.

### Competitive capability

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.009 -->
9. Freeze the strongest admissible 27B/31B open-model references and exact harnesses before final evaluation. Reference outputs remain evaluation evidence only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.010 -->
10. Execute the broad checkpoint-bound suite across language, mathematics, code, native reasoning, research, agents, structured tools, files/SQL, browser/UI, computer use, image, audio, multimodal composition, memory, world models, and interactive environments.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.011 -->
11. Define the normalized primary aggregate and domain composites before scores are visible. The lower 95% confidence bound of Ember's primary aggregate must be no more than 2% below the strongest frozen reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.012 -->
12. No required domain composite may fall below 90% of the weaker applicable 27B/31B reference. A strong text average cannot hide weak image, audio, reasoning, or tools.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.013 -->
13. Run matched dense restart, schedule growth, and ungated expert accumulation under the same tokens/FLOPs and analysis used at earlier rungs.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.014 -->
14. Require the accretion-versus-control advantage to exceed the frozen 15B effect.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.015 -->
15. Delete promoted experts, routes, memory/world-model state, consolidation objects, and tool/reasoning pathways according to the preregistered causal map.

### Organism and body

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.016 -->
16. Run sustained ember-cli goals in which the exact final checkpoint converses, reasons, uses tools, remembers, dreams, operates worlds, conducts research, diagnoses failures, and resumes after interruption.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.017 -->
17. Re-run operator relationship and trust tests at final capability; increased intelligence does not inherit broader authority automatically.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.WORK.018 -->
18. Verify that removing outside AI development agents, network access, subscriptions, and borrowed reference seats does not prevent normal local operation, research, learning, or recovery.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Counting allocated, stored, quantized, or rarely routed parameters that lack meaningful training and utilization.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Stopping after a 30-35B smoke schedule or extrapolating capability from 15B.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Using smaller-model results, scaling laws, loss alone, synthetic tasks, or harness readiness as competitive evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Averaging away a modality or native-reasoning failure.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Choosing weak or convenient reference models after seeing Ember's scores.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Letting active parameters, wall time, energy, host traffic, or hidden CPU work scale with total capacity while claiming nearly constant cost.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Calling PyTorch orchestration around borrowed critical primitives a fully owned creation stack without an exact boundary and nativization proof.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Using a borrowed model for task generation, evaluation judgment, filtering, debugging decisions, or operational cognition.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.001 -->
One public, independently reproducible final-model certificate must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.002 -->
1. More than 27,000,000,000 and within the frozen 30-35B target of unique total parameters, with exact active/trainable/served/trained counts.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.003 -->
2. Sufficient training, meaningful expert utilization, and clean lineage from the 15B organism.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.004 -->
3. Peak GPU memory at most 23.5 GiB, active trainable capacity at most 4B, and matched per-token cost upper bounds at most 1.25 times the 3B reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.005 -->
4. The primary aggregate is statistically non-inferior to the strongest frozen 27B/31B reference within the 2% margin, and every domain clears its 90% floor.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.006 -->
5. Native reasoning clears unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, multimodal, and tool-grounded tests after search, harness, and tool-policy ablations.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.007 -->
6. Verified Expert Accretion beats all matched controls and its advantage is statistically larger than at 15B.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.008 -->
7. Deletion and rollback causally bind promoted capability to the responsible model state.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.009 -->
8. The full owned stack creates, loads, serves, evaluates, and resumes the checkpoint from public recipes and receipted data.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.010 -->
9. The body, memory, world models, dreaming, tools, autonomous research, and operator relationship remain functional without material regression.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.CERT.011 -->
10. Normal operation succeeds without outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, borrowed references, or mandatory network access.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-09.REOPEN.001 -->
Any failed scale, cost, capability, lineage, ownership, modality, native-reasoning, or deletion leg keeps EMBER-09 active. A negative result constrains the tested final design but never converts the >27B target into an optional aspiration. Later contradictory evidence reopens this goal and invalidates dependent laboratory or release claims.
