# EMBER-02 — Three-billion-parameter foundation birth

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.OUTCOME.001 -->
Create the first model that may truthfully be called Ember: a sufficiently pretrained, clean-genesis, Ember-owned foundation model with at least 3,000,000,000 parameters, native text-image-audio processing, reasoning, structured tool use, plasticity, and a direct owned serving path.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.OUTCOME.002 -->
There is no smaller bootstrap-model milestone. Architecture, systems, data, and training research that requires a neural network runs at full 3B scale. Non-learning tensor and kernel fixtures may be smaller, but they cannot learn, checkpoint, serve, or carry capability claims.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.START.001 -->
- No current checkpoint qualifies. The Llama-style cbase line is constitutionally excluded and radically undertrained; the separate unified implementation lacks audio and full integration; borrowed Qwen is reference-only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.START.002 -->
- Exact-duplicate structural growth has already shown that nominally wider experts can remain identical and add no usable capacity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.START.003 -->
- Optimizer state and update work dominate measured current training time; memory traffic, data efficiency, training stability, capability per token, and whole-stack ownership remain open walls.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.START.004 -->
- The architectural properties recovered from the Gemma 4 study are evidence: raw image patches and audio frames projected into one decoder, soft-token splicing, multimodal-span attention, and 2D-capable positional reasoning. They do not authorize Gemma weights or identity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.START.005 -->
- BitNet, QAT, SubQ, MTP, iGRPO, RLM, GDN/Jet, Muon, TurboQuant, local updates, low-bit expert storage, routing, adapters, world-model interfaces, and sleep consolidation are conserved research variables, not a preselected checklist or discarded backlog.

## Required work

### Architecture by owned implementation

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.001 -->
1. Implement an Ember-owned unified decoder rather than adapting a Llama, Gemma, Qwen, or other family implementation as the backbone.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.002 -->
2. Project raw image patches and audio frames directly into the decoder token space. Use soft-token splicing, explicit multimodal spans, causal and span-aware attention, and 2D-capable positional reasoning without separate pretrained modality encoders.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.003 -->
3. Define one shared core plus differentiated expert-ready banks and task-level routing. Initialization must be asymmetric or partially reinitialized; exact expert twins are forbidden.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.004 -->
4. Make text, image, audio, reasoning, and tool trajectories part of one training representation and one checkpoint identity.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.005 -->
5. Bind parameter accounting for total, unique, active, trainable, served, and trained capacity. The first admissible configuration contains at least 3B unique parameters before any training step.

### Attack the local creation wall

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.006 -->
6. Measure the current full-shape 3B cost model before launch: bytes moved, optimizer state, active tensors, FLOPs/token, wall time/token, power, disk traffic, checkpoint cost, and recovery time.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.007 -->
7. Research the optimizer/update path as a first-class computation rather than a fixed framework primitive. Compare conventional reverse-mode plus optimizer, fused or local update, factored state, forward-with-update, precision-aware state, and other evidence-backed alternatives.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.008 -->
8. Translate inference optimizations into training hypotheses only when the training-axis mechanism and matched metric are explicit. Maintain the systematic inference-to-training research engine rather than isolated ports.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.009 -->
9. Implement and measure owned kernels, compiler passes, attention/state operations, numerics, storage, checkpointing, and `ember.cpp`-class runtime components where the live cost model shows leverage.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.010 -->
10. Test interactions among BitNet, QAT, SubQ, MTP, iGRPO, RLM, GDN, Muon, TurboQuant, routing, adapters, local credit, low-bit storage, and newly discovered mechanisms. One result cannot erase untested combinations or regimes.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.011 -->
11. Any neural discriminator used in this work is a full 3B candidate. Short candidates remain named research candidates and cannot become milestones through parameter count.

### Sovereign corpus and tokenizer

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.012 -->
12. Build the tokenizer and multimodal corpus from receipted human-created or deterministic sources with exact source, license, transformation, deduplication, contamination, ordering, and split custody.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.013 -->
13. Include broad language, mathematics, code, science, tools, files, SQL, browser/UI, images, audio, interactive worlds, and longitudinal research journals or process trajectories showing observation, hypothesis, action, evaluation, revision, preservation, and transfer.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.014 -->
14. Do not use any external model to generate, filter, rank, label, score, curate, order, stop, or otherwise shape a training token.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.015 -->
15. Freeze held-out and benchmark splits before training; prevent content, template, source-range, and packed-window leakage.

### Full pretraining and capability

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.016 -->
16. Freeze the strongest applicable open 2B-4B reference for each required capability domain and one normalized primary aggregate before training. Reference outputs remain evaluation evidence only and never enter training.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.017 -->
17. Freeze a sufficient-pretraining token budget, minimum-token floor, and stopping rule from the measured 3B learning curve before the claim-bearing run. Cost, loss plateau, or apparent convergence cannot relabel a checkpoint below the capability floors as fully trained.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.018 -->
18. Train on the single 24GB GPU with resumable exact-state checkpoints, deterministic recovery, resource guards, and receipted model/data/mechanism engagement.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.019 -->
19. Require the lower 95% confidence bound of the primary capability aggregate to reach at least 90% of its frozen 2B-4B reference and every required domain composite to reach at least 80% of its applicable reference and at least 2 times chance.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.020 -->
20. Test native reasoning on frozen unseen multi-step tasks spanning text, mathematics, code, image, audio, structured tools, and grounded interactive contexts. Require compositional generalization, counterfactual sensitivity, observable intermediate-action coherence, and causal degradation under responsible component deletion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.021 -->
21. Require the native-reasoning composite to reach at least 80% of its frozen reference and every reasoning category to reach at least 70%; fluent output, retrieval, scripted search, tool policy, or verifier behavior cannot supply the inference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.022 -->
22. Demonstrate useful cross-modal composition, not five isolated heads: text-guided image reasoning, audio-grounded reasoning, multimodal tool selection, and mixed-span generation or prediction.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.023 -->
23. Separate model reasoning from harness and tool execution with matched no-search, no-retrieval, restricted-tool, and deterministic-automation controls.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.024 -->
24. Demonstrate plasticity through a small verified post-pretraining update that produces held-out gain and rolls back when the update object is removed, without material unrelated regression.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.WORK.025 -->
25. Serve the exact checkpoint through a minimal owned endpoint and conduct the first recorded conversation and typed-tool action with identity visible. Full body parity belongs to EMBER-03.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Creating any trainable network below 3B, even as a “temporary seed,” “proxy model,” or schedule test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Initializing from current cbase, Qwen, Gemma, Llama, or any other borrowed weights.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Using an external model's outputs anywhere in the training-signal path.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Counting initialization, parameter allocation, a forward pass, a selftest, a short smoke run, falling loss alone, or endpoint loading as foundation-model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Calling separate image, audio, text, reasoning, or tool systems one unified model because a wrapper routes between them.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Deferring audio, reasoning, tools, memory interfaces, or the owned runtime to a later model generation.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Choosing an architecture because it already exists in the repository rather than because full-shape evidence supports it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Spending the run budget on repeated instrumentation repair while no bounded 3B measurement executes.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.001 -->
The first Ember checkpoint must pass one current, independently replayable certificate proving:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.002 -->
1. At least 3,000,000,000 unique model parameters and exact total/active/trainable/served/trained counts.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.003 -->
2. No ancestor weight tensor or model-mediated training signal outside the clean Ember genesis.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.004 -->
3. Exact architecture, tokenizer, corpus, order, optimizer, numeric, mechanism, checkpoint, and runtime hashes.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.005 -->
4. The frozen full token budget completed; an early statistical stop is admissible only after its frozen minimum-token floor and every capability floor below have cleared. Plateauing below a floor fails.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.006 -->
5. The primary aggregate lower 95% confidence bound is at least 90% of the frozen reference.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.007 -->
6. Every text, image, audio, structured-tool, and grounded-interaction domain composite lower bound is at least 80% of its applicable reference and at least 2 times chance.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.008 -->
7. The native-reasoning composite lower bound is at least 80% of its reference, no reasoning category is below 70%, and frozen unseen tasks cover text, mathematics, code, image, audio, tools, and grounded contexts.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.009 -->
8. Compositional generalization, counterfactual sensitivity, intermediate-action coherence, search/retrieval/tool ablations, and responsible component deletion causally bind reasoning to the owned checkpoint.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.010 -->
9. Cross-modal tests prove shared-decoder composition and deletion tests prove required modality and tool pathways are engaged.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.011 -->
10. The plasticity update improves its frozen held-out target, survives reload, reverses on deletion, and does not materially regress unrelated domains.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.012 -->
11. Peak GPU memory stays within the physical 24GB device and every resource claim is measured on the production path.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.013 -->
12. A clean process loads the checkpoint through the owned endpoint, reports the exact identity manifest, completes a recorded conversation and typed-tool action, and never labels a borrowed seat Ember.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.014 -->
13. An exact reproduction recipe can resume from genesis or a receipted checkpoint without hidden local state.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.CERT.015 -->
Only after all thirteen pass may this checkpoint become ember-cli's owned default or be called the first Ember model.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-02.REOPEN.001 -->
Negative architecture, optimizer, data, or capability results remain attached to the tested 3B regime. The goal stays active while the evidence graph selects new interactions or implementations; it does not retreat to a smaller network. If a later lineage, modality, or capability audit invalidates this birth certificate, EMBER-02 reopens and every later model claim loses authority until repaired.
