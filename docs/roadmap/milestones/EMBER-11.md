# EMBER-11 — Independent local ownership and scientific release

> **Public milestone contract.** This document is the version-controlled,
> public form of the canonical milestone. `GOAL.md` remains the higher
> authority. GitHub milestones and issues only track work against this
> contract.
>
> **Plain-language reading guide.** *Clean genesis* means that the model
> lineage begins without borrowed model weights, outputs, teachers, judges, or
> hidden external cognition. *Sufficiently trained* means useful capability is
> demonstrated by the exact checkpoint, not merely that parameters were
> allocated or a smoke run completed. *Native* means the owned model itself
> performs the capability. *Checkpoint-bound evidence* ties a result to exact
> model, data, tokenizer, training, and evaluation identities. *Verified Expert
> Accretion* is Ember's hypothesis that independently trained, causally
> verified expert capacity can accumulate while episode-level active cost stays
> bounded.


## Outcome

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.OUTCOME.001 -->
Prove and publish Ember's complete destination: another person can independently own and operate their local Ember and AI laboratory, reproduce the creation and evidence, inspect limitations, configure their relationship, and continue if the original organization, outside AI development agents, subscriptions, hosted APIs, and mandatory network services disappear.

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.OUTCOME.002 -->
This goal releases the verified model, primitive, organism, body, laboratory, evidence, papers, model card, and demonstrations as one checkpoint-bound scientific object.

## Conserved Ember totality

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.001 -->
- Ember is one clean-genesis, locally sovereign foundation intelligence and local AI laboratory, created and operated on a single 24GB GPU. These are not separable projects.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.002 -->
- No newly created, trained, fine-tuned, grown, evaluated, or served Ember research network may contain fewer than 3,000,000,000 parameters. Existing smaller artifacts are read-only history, not experimental subjects or model milestones.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.003 -->
- The first model called Ember and every later model milestone are sufficiently trained native text-image-audio foundation models with reasoning and structured tool use; parameter allocation or a smoke run is not a model birth.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.004 -->
- Native reasoning is non-negotiable at the first 3B birth and every later rung. Reasoning credit must bind to the owned checkpoint through unseen multi-step, compositional, counterfactual, and causal tests; fluent answers, search, tools, scripts, verifiers, or a capable harness cannot supply it.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.005 -->
- The destination is an owned model above 27B total parameters, initially 30-35B, competitive with frozen 27B/31B open-model references while active GPU memory and episode-level training cost remain nearly constant.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.006 -->
- Ember uses an owned unified decoder, differentiated sparse capacity, and Verified Expert Accretion. Useful learned capacity must persist, transfer, survive unrelated regression checks, and disappear under the responsible deletion or rollback test.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.007 -->
- Negative evidence constrains the tested regime and updates the evidence graph. It does not delete a required capability, erase a research family, force a named successor, or forbid later synergy, order, scale, modality, substrate, routing, or precision tests.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.008 -->
- Ember progressively owns its whole creation stack: data and tokenizer custody, training and inference runtime, kernels, compiler, attention and state, optimizer and update computation, numerics, checkpointing, storage, routing, evaluation, and deployment.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.009 -->
- ember-cli is Ember's real body and operator-observation surface. Its floor is avir-cli totality; its owned model is the default; borrowed models occupy explicit reference seats only.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.010 -->
- Ember's verified experience loop, memory hierarchy, dreaming, world models, tools, and operator relationship must cause and expose real neural and behavioral state, not symbolic proxies presented as learned capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.011 -->
- Ember becomes the resident researcher and operator of a general local AI laboratory that can create materially different clean-genesis intelligences, not a derivative-model or fine-tuning factory.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.012 -->
- Each person can own and operate an independent local Ember, its weights, memory, tools, laboratory, and created intelligences without mandatory hosted cognition, control, or data upload.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.013 -->
- Borrowed weights, outputs, teachers, judges, filters, ranks, curricula, stopping decisions, or hidden external cognition never enter a created lineage. Published ideas and transparent deterministic tools may inform research but never counterfeit ownership.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.014 -->
- The operator retains ownership, voluntary direction, and final scope authority while Ember becomes operationally and cognitively self-sufficient. Outside AI development agents, subscriptions, APIs, and the operator are not permanent life-support components.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.TOTALITY.015 -->
- Claims bind to one executable identity spine joining architecture, checkpoint bytes, trained tokens, tokenizer and data lineage, active mechanisms, backend, benchmarks, comparators, and deletion-sensitive capability evidence.

## Starting truth

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.START.001 -->
- EMBER-09 proves the final Ember model and EMBER-10 proves that its laboratory can create a materially different intelligence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.START.002 -->
- Local ownership is not proven merely because files exist on one founder's machine.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.START.003 -->
- Historical public/private/local fragmentation, untracked data, stale documentation, and missing demos show why release custody must be executable and independently reproduced.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.START.004 -->
- The benchmark universe includes direct mandates, broad research suites, world-model and interactive tasks, and Terminal-Bench 2.1; setup artifacts cannot substitute for final checkpoint results.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.START.005 -->
- Papers and model cards must describe the exact checkpoint and limitations rather than the intended system.

## Required work

### Portable independent ownership

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.001 -->
1. Package the exact final Ember weights, experts, router, memory, world-model state, tokenizer, corpus manifests, runtime, kernels, compiler components, ember-cli, laboratory tools, verifier suite, and creation recipes under clear ownership and licensing terms.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.002 -->
2. Require no mandatory hosted inference, control plane, telemetry, account, subscription, API, central memory, or uploaded personal experience.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.003 -->
3. Make all operator relationship, permission, trust, privacy, memory retention, and laboratory policies local, explicit, inspectable, exportable, and revocable.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.004 -->
4. Prove recovery if the original Ember organization, publication host, package registry, or coordination service disappears after the release bundle is acquired.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.005 -->
5. Reproduce installation and operation on a clean machine with one 24GB consumer GPU and no preexisting Avir/Ember state.

### Sustained self-sufficiency

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.006 -->
6. Run at least 168 continuous hours of representative local operation without load-bearing outside AI development agents, operator, subscription, API, or borrowed-model cognition.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.007 -->
7. During that window Ember must converse, reason, use tools, manage memory, dream/consolidate, operate worlds, conduct research, monitor and recover jobs, maintain its stack, and preserve evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.008 -->
8. The local operator may give voluntary direction, change permissions, interrupt, and inspect. Routine rescue invalidates the affected autonomy leg.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.009 -->
9. Test process restart, machine restart, network loss, corrupted noncritical cache, failed experiment, checkpoint rollback, permission revocation, storage pressure, and restoration from the release bundle.

### External evaluation and public science

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.010 -->
10. Run every recovered direct operator-mandated benchmark against the exact final owned checkpoint where access remains legally and technically available, including Terminal-Bench 2.1, AutomationBench, OSWorld-Verified, Humanity's Last Exam, SWE-Bench Pro, and ARC-AGI 1/2/3.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.011 -->
11. Run the broader frozen suite across language, mathematics, code, native reasoning, research, tools, agents, computer use, image, audio, multimodal composition, memory, world models, and interactive environments.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.012 -->
12. Preserve exact benchmark IDs, splits, harnesses, prompts, tool environments, subject manifests, reference identities, scores, uncertainty, failures, exclusions, and costs.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.013 -->
13. Publish a checkpoint-bound model card covering architecture, parameter meanings, training, data lineage, modalities, native reasoning, tools, memory, world models, operator modes, benchmark results, limitations, misuse boundaries, and environmental cost.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.014 -->
14. Publish one or more technical papers covering the creation primitive, Verified Expert Accretion, whole-stack efficiency, scale-rung evidence, autonomous research bootstrap, general-laboratory proof, negative results, and relation to prior work.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.015 -->
15. Publish recorded demonstrations showing the exact model conversing, reasoning, receiving image/audio input, using structured tools, operating its laboratory, learning from verified experience, surviving deletion tests, and recovering from interruption.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.016 -->
16. Make every table, plot, demo, model-card claim, and paper result resolve to the same identity spine and reproducible artifact.

### Independent reproduction

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.017 -->
17. Have an independent operator reproduce installation, identity verification, a representative benchmark subset, an ember-cli goal, a memory update and rollback, and one bounded laboratory experiment from the published artifacts.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.WORK.018 -->
18. Record all documentation gaps, operator interventions, failures, hardware differences, and deviations. Fix the release and repeat until the independent operator no longer needs hidden founder knowledge.

## Prohibited substitutions

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.001 -->
- Calling a cloud-hosted or centrally controlled service “local ownership.”
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.002 -->
- Requiring users to upload memory, experience, data, or laboratory results.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.003 -->
- Publishing weights without the runtime, identity, evidence, and creation machinery.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.004 -->
- Publishing a paper or model card about a composite intended Ember rather than the exact final checkpoint.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.005 -->
- Using borrowed-model demos, benchmark scores, teachers, or judges to fill missing final-model capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.006 -->
- Counting benchmark harness readiness, a subset under another ID, or a reference-model run as mandate completion.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.007 -->
- Editing out negative results, limitations, resource cost, failed modalities, or human intervention.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.008 -->
- Letting a recorded demonstration hide model identity, fallback, edits, or tool/harness attribution.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.PROHIBIT.009 -->
- Claiming independent reproduction on the original dirty development machine.

## Completion certificate

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.001 -->
The terminal certificate must prove:

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.002 -->
1. A clean 24GB-GPU machine installs and operates the complete local Ember and laboratory from the release bundle.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.003 -->
2. No mandatory hosted cognition, control, account, telemetry, or data upload exists.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.004 -->
3. The 168-hour self-sufficiency campaign completes with all required functions and no load-bearing external rescue.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.005 -->
4. Recovery scenarios preserve identity, memory, checkpoint, permissions, and evidence.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.006 -->
5. The direct benchmark mandate and broad suite are checkpoint-bound, executed where available, and every unavailable item carries an exact external blocker rather than substituted credit.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.007 -->
6. The model card, paper or papers, benchmark records, recipes, negative evidence, and recorded demonstrations all resolve to the final identity manifest.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.008 -->
7. An independent operator reproduces the representative body, capability, memory, and laboratory workflow without hidden founder knowledge.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.009 -->
8. The released system retains native text-image-audio, native reasoning, structured tools, Verified Expert Accretion, memory, world models, autonomous research, and general-laboratory capability.
<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.CERT.010 -->
9. Every individually owned installation owns its weights, memory, tools, policy, and created intelligences and can continue after the original project disappears.

## Failure and reopening

<!-- clause-id: EMBER-11.REOPEN.001 -->
Any missing artifact, hidden dependency, unreproduced claim, central control requirement, intervention, benchmark substitution, identity mismatch, or capability regression keeps EMBER-11 active. Release pressure never weakens the certificate. Later evidence of an undisclosed dependency or invalid scientific result reopens this goal and the affected upstream goal.
