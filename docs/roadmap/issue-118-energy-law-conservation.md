# Issue #118 current-scale conservation ruling

Status: `SUPERSEDED_NOT_PLANNED`

Issue #118 froze a 2026 W1/C14-era path for moving the energy-law program from
unevaluable receipts to measured experiments. Two bookkeeping clauses were
already completed in the issue itself: the derived W1 envelope point was
banked, and the unreachable three-window ECON-PASS floor was repaired. Those
facts do not make the remaining scientific program complete.

The historical execution subjects are now inadmissible. Current Ember requires
a clean-genesis 3B foundation and routes launches, identity, resource evidence,
and receipts through Ember Lab and the governed custody spine. Re-running W1,
c03, h2048, or another sub-3B C14 subject would violate that trajectory and
would not provide current model evidence.

https://github.com/wordingone/ember/issues/1116#issuecomment-5221234668. It
already owns the eight-point 3B energy/resource envelope transferred from #7.

1. a preregistered H-MLI null/lever experiment with matched seed-varied arms,
   fixed token axis, declared smoothing and equivalence rules, and retained

   limit, Joules, tokens, active FLOPs, MFU, VRAM, wall time, and exact

   magnitude, with a frozen fit family, fit/holdout split, confidence
   intervals, residuals, and outliers;
4. H-SHAPE measurements on the admissible current 3B architecture, including
   uncertainty and a preregistered verdict;
5. the fixed-work board-power-cap comparison, only after an exact same-run
   baseline, with identical owned lineage, data order, optimizer, batch path,
   and target rule; Joules-to-target, wall-time-to-target, and
   active-FLOPs-to-target remain separate outcomes;
6. fail-closed sampler behavior, no target-crossing overclaim, negative-result
   retention, rollback, and no capability or sufficient-pretraining credit.

Issue #869 may provide a future same-run microbatch baseline, and EMBER-05/#1119
may later consume an established envelope for earned-growth work. Neither is a
