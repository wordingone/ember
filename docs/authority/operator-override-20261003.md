## Scope

Third-party AI-generated or mixed datasets only, never self-generated. Nothing generated by any team seat, or by any model the team runs (including a locally run generator), is ever admitted for training. An admissible dataset is existing, published and pre-compiled, and its receipt carries a complete block: synthetic: true, origin_class (known_ai | mixed | unknown), dataset id, revision, the dataset's own provenance reference, licence reference, AI-made share (null only for unknown origin), terms_permit_training: true, self_generated: false. Known AI origin and unknown origin are counted as separate classes. Human-sourced data stays the first priority; AI-made or mixed datasets are not sought ahead of it. Borrowed weights, judges and teacher-in-the-loop training remain forbidden. Held-out and protected evaluation remain human-sourced.

## Consequence, stated openly

From the first lineage update that consumes a marked row, the lineage's training-signal path includes AI-generated data. Capabilities from that update onward are not claimed as clause-3 creation. Every later receipt, manifest and claim carries training_signal_provenance (human_only | includes_ai_generated) and the AI-made share. Continuity of the record is kept and disclosed, never hidden.

## Non-dependence

1. Retention. After consumption of marked rows stops, at least 80% of the human-only held-out gain attributed to them must be retained across the next 3 lineage hours. Falling short counts as dependence: it is reported, and the cap is not raised. The reliability seat runs this check in the first hour after consumption stops; a first-hour reading is interim only.
2. Ratchet. The pilot ceiling is 10% of applied targets per lineage hour. The cap only moves down; any raise needs a recorded ruling based on measured learning per token, AI-made and human separately.
3. No teacher at use. Inference, evaluation and every selection or stopping decision never call a teacher model.

## Duration

Until Ember's self-improving capacity is proven by the contract's criterion; then new AI-made data stops unless the operator renews this.
