## Scope

Quality, not origin, is the admission gate for every source: executed checks, deduplication, a contamination screen against protected sets, and licence terms that permit training. Third-party AI-generated or mixed datasets only, never self-generated. Nothing generated by any team seat, or by any model the team runs (including a locally run generator), is ever admitted for training. An admissible dataset is existing, published and pre-compiled, and its receipt carries a complete block: synthetic: true, origin_class (known_ai | mixed | unknown), dataset id, revision, the dataset's own provenance reference, licence reference, content sha256 equal to the fetched bytes, AI-made share (null only for unknown origin), terms_permit_training: true, self_generated: false, and the receipt statement "AI-generated: <dataset>@<revision>" in place of the human-only L3 attestation. Human lots keep the L3 attestation unchanged. Known AI origin and unknown origin are counted as separate classes. Human-sourced data stays the first priority; AI-made or mixed datasets are not sought ahead of it. Borrowed weights, judges and teacher-in-the-loop training remain forbidden. Evaluation populations are kept free of known AI-generated items, which are excluded before the frozen eligible population is drawn; unknown origin is never counted as human. Purity beyond that is not claimed.

## Consequence, stated openly

From the first lineage update that consumes a marked row, the lineage's training-signal path includes AI-generated data. Capabilities from that update onward are not claimed as clause-3 creation. ## Non-dependence

1. Retention (observability). After consumption of marked rows stops, at least 80% of the held-out gain on evaluations kept free of known AI-generated items attributed to them must be retained across the next 3 lineage hours. Falling short counts as dependence: it is reported, and the cap is not raised. The reliability seat runs this check in the first hour after consumption stops; a first-hour reading is interim only.
2. Share and ceiling. The AI-made share is reported every lineage hour. Pilot ceiling: at most 10% of applied targets per lineage hour and 20M unique admitted targets combined across all known_ai, mixed and unknown lots. The entire unknown-origin slice and any mixed slice without exact row/token origin charge the ceiling in full. Raises are ruled from measured learning per token, AI-made and human separately.
3. No teacher at use (observability). Inference, evaluation and every selection or stopping decision never call a teacher model.
