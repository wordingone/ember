# Ember glossary

**Admitted.** Accepted by the governing verifier and independent review for the exact claimed use.
Implemented, executed, or measured does not imply admitted.

**Canonical document.** The one current document that owns a concept. Other current pages link to
it; superseded and historical pages are visibly non-current.

**Claim source.** A public authority, implementation, execution receipt, explicit target, or
editorial/navigation source bound to a stable claim ID and content hash.

**Clean genesis.** Ember's target lineage excludes borrowed learned or evaluative signals. Frozen
references may be used only within the governing authority boundary.

**Ember Lab.** The cross-domain integration and experiment surface. Lab uses declared domain
interfaces and does not replace their ownership.

**Receipt.** Machine-readable evidence emitted by an executed process and checked against its
contract. A receipt proves only its bound fields and claim boundary.

**Target-declared.** A destination interface or outcome is specified, but implementation,
execution, measurement, independent reproduction, or admission may still be absent.

For exact mutable definitions and execution state, use
[`docs/domains/governance/authority/CONTINUITY.md`](domains/governance/authority/CONTINUITY.md).
