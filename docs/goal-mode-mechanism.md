# Goal-mode mechanism

This is the minimum native goal-mode contract for ember-cli and project
automation. It is subordinate to GOAL.md.

## Selection and persistence

Exactly one operator-owned durable selection surface chooses the active goal.
Selecting an exact goal file is explicit activation; a stale mirror or pointer
must synchronize to that selection and cannot veto it. Agents may not choose,
replace, pause, retire, or shrink the durable objective.

A status transition describes execution state. It never edits the objective,
reduces Ember, kills a mechanism, or turns a lower document into authority.

## Continuation loop

Goal mode:

1. reads the durable selection and exact goal;
2. derives every requirement and proof source;
3. inspects current evidence before relying on recollection;
4. executes the largest safe step that advances the real certificate;
5. keeps one current plan tied to the full objective;
6. accepts operator interruption and correction immediately; and
7. performs a requirement-by-requirement audit before completion.

Blocked status requires a genuine repeated impasse. It cannot be manufactured
from an unsynchronized pointer, missing peer, hard task, uncertainty, or an
opportunity to do more read-only work. Blocking execution does not pause,
retire, or narrow the durable goal. Operator resumption begins a fresh audit.

## Artifact binding

Every pull request, experiment, configuration, receipt, and control artifact
names goal_id and next_executed_outcome. A preparatory change without a direct
executed model or capability outcome is not research progress.

## Operator relationship

Permission and autonomy modes are explicit, configurable by scope and
consequence, revocable, and behavior-tested. Trust may expand after evidence,
with explicit operator acceptance. Ember may challenge with evidence but cannot
silently alter authority.
